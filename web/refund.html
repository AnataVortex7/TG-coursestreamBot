Refund & Payment Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

Please read this policy carefully before making a payment for any course on Akshayclasses.

1. Payment Process

Payments for course access are accepted as instructed during the purchase process. Once your payment is submitted and verified by our team, course access will be granted to your account.

⚠️ Important: After submitting your payment, access may take 24 to 48 hours to be activated. Please do not raise a dispute or claim regarding your payment during this window. Doing so before access is granted may result in your request being cancelled.

2. Refund Policy

All purchases made on Akshayclasses are non-refundable by default. Once course access has been granted to your account, we do not offer automatic refunds.

Refund requests are considered on a case-by-case basis, entirely at the discretion of Akshayclasses. If you believe you have a genuine reason for a refund, you may submit your request along with full details using the Contact Us form available after logging in. We will review and respond at our earliest convenience.

Factors that will not be considered valid grounds for a refund include, but are not limited to:

  • Change of mind after access has been granted.
  • Not using the course after access was given.
  • Account suspension due to violation of our Terms & Conditions (e.g. account sharing, content recording/distribution).

3. Access Validity

Course access is granted for the duration specified at the time of purchase. Access may be revoked without refund if these Terms are violated.

4. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy at any time at our sole discretion, without prior notice. Continued use of the platform after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

5. Contact Us

For any payment or refund queries, please use the Contact Us form available after logging in.
